M1 — Listening

Structure lessons, teach prediction, and train paraphrase recognition.

Lesson plan (60–75 min)

  • 5 min — Topic vocabulary warm‑up
  • 10 min — Predict answers from question types
  • 25 min — Play audio once; answer all; skip if missed
  • 10 min — Check; map synonyms; paraphrase table
  • 10 min — Replay hard parts; numbers/dates practice

Techniques

  • Underline keywords; guess word type (noun, number, adj)
  • Paraphrase bank: buy/purchase; go up/increase; about/approximately
  • Spelling traps: address, accommodation, environment, necessary
  • Map questions to sections before listening

Homework ideas

  • Listen with transcript 10 min daily; shadow for pronunciation
  • Make a personal synonyms list per practice test
  • Numbers dictation: prices, phone numbers, dates
