Real-life Scenarios

Teach IELTS skills using everyday problems. Each scenario includes tasks, sample answers, and tricks.

University application

Speaking Part 2

Describe a time you applied for a course or training. You should say…

Sample (Band 7): I applied for a data course last year. First, I… Then, I… Finally, I was accepted because… Overall, it helped me…

  • Use past simple for sequence; link with first, then, after that, finally
  • Add one example detail for each step

Writing Task 2

Some people think university should be free. Do you agree or disagree?

Mini-model: In my view, tuition fees should be reduced but not removed, mainly because funding is limited and quality may fall. For example… Overall…

  • Clear position in intro and conclusion
  • Reason + example per body paragraph

Reading & Listening

Find key information in an admissions email; note deadlines and documents.

  • Scan for dates, numbers, required items
  • Underline paraphrases: submit/provide; deadline/closing date

Housing rental call

Listening form

Note name, price, location, move‑in date. Drill numbers and spelling.

Speaking Part 3

Why do people rent rather than buy? Sample: Renting is flexible, mainly because… For instance… So overall…

Tricks

  • Predict word type before listening
  • Confirm numbers; repeat back

Workplace meeting

Speaking Part 2

Describe a productive meeting. Use problem → solution → result.

Writing (email)

Politely request a change: purpose, reason, specific ask, thanks.

Tricks

  • Chunking for fluency
  • Formal tone for emails

Health appointment

Listening

Clinic call with dates and times; practice calendar language.

Speaking Part 1

Health habits: give short answer + one detail. E.g., I walk daily — usually after dinner — because…

Tricks

  • Numbers and time phrases
  • Paraphrase symptoms simply
