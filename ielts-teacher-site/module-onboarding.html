What is IELTS?

IELTS tests English in 4 skills: Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking. There are Academic and General Training versions. Scores are 0–9 bands in 0.5 steps.

  • Listening: 40 questions from 4 recordings
  • Reading: 40 questions from 3 long texts (Academic) or 3 sections (GT)
  • Writing: Task 1 (report/letter) + Task 2 (essay)
  • Speaking: 3 parts interview with an examiner

Band scores explained simply

  • Band 6: generally clear but with mistakes; ideas sometimes basic
  • Band 7: clear, organized, good vocabulary; some natural errors
  • Band 8: very clear and accurate; strong control and precise words

We use simplified checklists in teaching. Full public descriptors are available from official sources.

Your training roadmap

  • Week 1: Watch model lessons for each skill; try teaching 1 friend
  • Week 2: Learn writing templates; mark 3 essays using rubric
  • Week 3: Speaking drills; pronunciation and fluency practice
  • Week 4: Mock tests; collect testimonials; start paid trials

Mindset for Asian teachers

  • Your accent is okay. Aim for clarity of sounds and word stress.
  • Use short chunks. Avoid over‑complicated grammar when teaching.
  • Focus your students on ideas, structure, and clear language.
