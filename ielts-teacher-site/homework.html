Homework Bank — Practical Tasks

Short, realistic tasks that build exam skills.

Education

  • Write an email to ask about a course (purpose, question, thanks)
  • Read a course page; list 3 requirements and 2 deadlines
  • Speaking: record 60s about a helpful teacher (2 small stories)

Housing

  • Listening: fill a rental application with names, dates, prices
  • Writing: describe differences between two apartments (Task 1 style)
  • Speaking: pros and cons of living near work (reason + example)
