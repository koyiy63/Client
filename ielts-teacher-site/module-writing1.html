M3 — Writing Task 1

Charts, tables, processes, maps. Teach overview and grouped details.

Template

4‑paragraph frame

Intro: paraphrase topic.

Overview: 2–3 key trends.

Details 1: group A with comparisons.

Details 2: group B with comparisons.

Lesson flow (60 min)

  • 10 min — Identify chart type; pick 2–3 trends
  • 15 min — Plan overview and grouping
  • 25 min — Write full report with time
  • 10 min — Use rubric to give feedback
