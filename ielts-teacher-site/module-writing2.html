M4 — Writing Task 2

Teach a 5‑paragraph essay with position, reasons, examples, and short conclusion.

Template

Essay frame

Intro: paraphrase + thesis.

Body 1: reason 1 + example.

Body 2: reason 2 + example.

Conclusion: restate and short wrap‑up.

Marking focus (Band‑style)

  • Task Response: clear position; relevant, developed ideas
  • Coherence & Cohesion: paragraphing; reference words
  • Lexical Resource: topic vocabulary; collocations
  • Grammar: accurate simple + some complex sentences
