Q&A Bank — Speaking Follow-ups

Use opinion → reason → example → wrap‑up. Keep answers organized.

Education

  • Should homework be limited? Band 6: It should be balanced because… For example… So overall…
  • Are exams the best way to assess? Band 7: Not always, mainly because… For instance… Therefore…

Technology

  • Does social media improve communication? 6: Sometimes yes… 7: It depends; while it helps…, it can also…
  • Should companies allow remote work? 6: Yes, because… 7: It increases productivity if…

Health

  • How can cities promote exercise? 6: Build parks… 7: Provide safe lanes and community programs…
  • Do people eat healthier now? 6: Not really, because… 7: Trends are mixed; although…, many still…
