M5 — Speaking

Fast Q&A, story‑based Part 2, and reason‑example answers for Part 3.

Lesson flow (45–60 min)

  • Part 1: short answers + one detail
  • Part 2: 1‑minute notes; tell 2–3 small stories
  • Part 3: opinion → reason → example → mini‑conclusion

Pronunciation focus

  • Word stress and sentence stress
  • Linking and intonation
  • Common L1 issues (r/l; final sounds; long/short vowels)
