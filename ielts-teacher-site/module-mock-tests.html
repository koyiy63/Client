M9 — Mock Tests & Timing

Use timers and reflection to build test stamina.

Timers

  • Listening: ~30 minutes
  • Reading: 60 minutes
  • Writing: 60 minutes (20 + 40)
  • Speaking: 11–14 minutes

After-test analysis

  • Review wrong answers: reason and fix
  • Collect paraphrases and topic vocabulary
  • Update personal error list (grammar/spelling)
