Diagnostic & Intake — First Lesson

Use this script and mini‑tests to set a baseline and plan the course.

Intake script (10 minutes)

  • Goal: Band X by [date]; Academic or General?
  • Previous scores; strongest/weakest skill
  • Schedule and homework capacity (days/time)

Quick diagnostic tasks (20 minutes)

  • Speaking: Part 1 Qs (3–4); note fluency and clarity
  • Writing: Task 2 intro + one body (10 min)
  • Reading: 5 T/F/NG questions
  • Listening: short form — names, dates, numbers

Error checklist

  • Articles/plurals, prepositions, verb tense control
  • Pronunciation: word stress, final sounds
  • Cohesion: paragraphs, reference words

Band estimate & plan

  • Give a conservative band range (e.g., 6.0–6.5)
  • Set 4‑week plan and homework routine
  • Schedule next lesson and mock test date
