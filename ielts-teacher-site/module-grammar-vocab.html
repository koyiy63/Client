M6 — Grammar & Vocabulary

Fix high‑impact issues and grow useful topic vocabulary.

Grammar priorities

  • Articles and plurals (a/an, the, zero article)
  • Prepositions: depend on, responsible for, interested in
  • Tense consistency in stories and processes
  • Sentence structure: subject‑verb agreement

Vocabulary building

  • Topic banks: education, environment, technology, health
  • Collocations: make a decision, have an impact, take measures
  • Word formation: improve → improvement, decide → decision
