M2 — Reading

Timing is everything. Teach skim/scan, T/F/NG logic, and keyword paraphrase.

Lesson plan (60–75 min)

  • 10 min — Skim titles, headings, first sentences
  • 20 min — Passage practice with time limit
  • 15 min — T/F/NG logic + matching headings
  • 15 min — Vocabulary mining and collocations

Techniques

  • T/F/NG: If the text does not state it, it is Not Given
  • Underline names, dates, numbers; they anchor answers
  • Map paraphrase: cause/lead to; benefit/advantage
