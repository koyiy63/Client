Writing Models — Task 1 & 2

Short, clear models focusing on band features.

Task 1 (line graph)

Overview: Overall, sales rose steadily, with a brief dip in 2018.

Details: From 2015 to 2017, figures increased from 20 to 40. In 2018, they fell slightly to 38, before peaking at 55 in 2019.

  • Report trends; avoid reasons not shown
  • Use approximations and comparison language

Task 2 (opinion)

Intro: Many argue that remote work boosts productivity. In my view, it often does, mainly due to flexible schedules and fewer commutes.

Body 1: Flexibility allows focused hours. For example…

Body 2: Less commuting saves energy and time. For instance…

Conclusion: Overall, when supported by clear goals, remote work can be highly effective.

  • Clear position; reasons + examples
  • Reference words for cohesion (this trend, such benefits)
