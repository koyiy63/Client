Mock Test — Full Set

Use a timer. Check answers after finishing each section.

Listening (script — 10 questions)

Script (read aloud): Good morning. I'd like to book a city tour on June 24 for three adults and one child. We prefer the morning slot. My contact number is 07921 555 843, and my email is lee.han@example.com. Please confirm the meeting point near River Street and the total cost including the child's discount.

Answer key
1 Date: June 24; 2 People: 3 adults + 1 child; 3 Time: morning; 4 Phone: 07921 555 843; 5 Email: lee.han@example.com; 6 Meeting point: River Street; 7 Cost: total with child discount; 8 Preference: morning slot; 9 Contact method: email; 10 Item: city tour.

Trick: Predict word type; confirm numbers; spell names back.

Reading (short passage — 10 questions)

Passage: Urban gardens are growing in popularity as residents convert rooftops and empty lots into green spaces. These gardens can lower temperatures, absorb rainfall, and provide fresh produce. However, success depends on community involvement and simple maintenance plans.

Questions + key
1 Purpose of gardens (multi choice): reduce heat / manage rain / grow food — All. 2 T/F/NG: Rooftop gardens are the only type — F. 3 Benefit for rainfall — absorb. 4 Challenge mentioned — community involvement. 5 Key to success — simple maintenance. 6 Paraphrase: fresh produce = food. 7 Names/numbers present? — No. 8 Which spaces are converted? — rooftops and empty lots. 9 Main topic — urban gardens. 10 Tone — positive but practical.

Trick: Skim first; then scan for keywords and paraphrases.

Writing

Task 1 (Academic)

The chart shows the percentage of households using three energy sources from 2000 to 2020. Summarise by selecting and reporting main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Task 1 (General)

You recently moved to a new city and want to join a local sports club. Write a letter asking for information about membership, schedules, and beginner classes.

Task 2

Some people argue that cities should invest more in public parks than in shopping centres. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Banding guide (short)
Task 1: Overview with 2–3 trends; group data; comparisons; accurate language.
Task 2: Clear position; reason + example per body; cohesive reference words; precise vocabulary.

Speaking set

Part 1: hometown, daily routine, hobbies

Part 2: Describe a project you completed successfully. You should say…

Part 3: teamwork, leadership, evaluation of success

Sample structures (Band 6/7)
6: Short answers + one detail in P1; 3‑step story in P2; simple opinion‑reason‑example in P3.
7: P1 with natural expansion; P2 using problem → solution → result; P3 with balanced viewpoints and mini conclusions.
