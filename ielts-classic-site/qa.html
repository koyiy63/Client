Q&A Bank — Speaking follow‑ups

Structure every answer: opinion → reason → example → wrap‑up.

Education

Should homework be limited?
Band 6: It should be balanced because too much causes stress; for example, students may sleep late. So a moderate amount is better.
Band 7: Limits make sense when homework repeats classwork without feedback. For instance, shorter tasks with clear objectives improve learning while preserving rest.
Are exams the best way to assess?
Band 6: Not always, because some students get nervous. For example, projects can show skills differently.
Band 7: Exams measure recall under time pressure, but coursework captures research and collaboration. A mix provides a fairer picture overall.
Should university be free?
Band 6: It would help poor families, but budgets are limited.
Band 7: Free tuition expands access, yet sustainable funding requires caps or targeted support to protect quality.

Technology

Does social media improve communication?
Band 6: Sometimes yes, because it is fast. For example, I share updates quickly.
Band 7: It increases reach but can reduce depth; scheduled calls or long messages often improve understanding.
Should companies allow remote work?
Band 6: Yes, because it saves time.
Band 7: It can raise productivity if teams set goals, track outcomes, and meet regularly to prevent isolation.
Are smartphones making people less focused?
Band 6: A bit, because of notifications.
Band 7: Attention suffers without boundaries; batching messages and using focus modes helps.

Work & Economy

Is job security more important than salary?
Band 6: For many people, yes; feeling safe matters.
Band 7: It depends on life stage; early careers may prioritise pay, while parents often value stability for planning.
Should governments support small businesses?
Band 6: Yes, because they create jobs.
Band 7: Targeted grants and training can boost local economies, but support should reward innovation and transparency.
Is lifelong learning necessary?
Band 6: Yes, technology changes quickly.
Band 7: Skills expire faster; micro‑courses and on‑the‑job projects keep workers adaptable.

Health & Lifestyle

How can cities promote exercise?
Band 6: Build parks and bike lanes.
Band 7: Safe routes and community programs increase participation, especially when combined with lighting and weekend events.
Do people eat healthier now?
Band 6: Not really; fast food is common.
Band 7: Trends are mixed; while awareness is higher, time pressure still drives processed choices.
Is mental health discussed openly?
Band 6: More than before.
Band 7: Stigma is falling, but access remains uneven; workplaces need training and clear referral paths.
